# Human Writing Guidelines (Anti-AI Content Field Guide)
*Derived from [Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing)*

## Purpose
Large Language Models (LLMs) have characteristic phrasing, rhetorical patterns, and structural tropes that make content immediately recognizable as machine-generated. This skill establishes rigorous standards to ensure all website copy, project descriptions, corporate literature, and UI text remain authentic, grounded, human, and legally credible.

---

Never use these over-indexed machine vocabulary words and stock clichés:

| **Tapestry** | *"A rich tapestry of heritage and modern living"* | *"Residential plots near GT Road and Gayatri Mandir"* |

## 2. Structural & Rhetorical AI Tropes to Ban

### A. The "Rule of Three" Formula
- **AI Pattern**: Habitual grouping of three adjectives or nouns: *"trust, innovation, and sustainability"*, *"timeless, elegant, and sophisticated"*.
- **Fix**: Use specific, uneven, factual statements. Say what the company actually does without forcing poetic triplets.

### B. Negative Parallelisms
- **AI Pattern**: *"Not only a home, but a lifestyle."*, *"Not just land, but your family's future."*, *"It is not merely an investment, but rather a journey."*
- **Fix**: State the concrete truth directly: *"Clear-title residential plots available in Aurangabad."*

### C. Undue Grandiosity and Significance Inflation
- **AI Pattern**: Making routine business operations sound world-historical: *"Marking a historic milestone in the regional trajectory of Bihar's real estate evolution."*
- **Fix**: Ground statements in practical local realities: *"Verified plots located in Aurangabad, with direct connectivity to Grand Trunk Road (NH-19)."*

### D. Canned Sycophancy & Artificial Praise
- **AI Pattern**: *"Renowned visionary leaders with unmatched acumen and esteemed heritage."*
- **Fix**: Factual corporate identity: *"Promoted by Kundan Kumar Singh and Vikas Kumar Singh, operators of the landmark Siyaram shop in Aurangabad."*

### E. Outline-Style Wrap-Up Summaries
- **AI Pattern**: Ending sections with: *"In summary, as Bihar strides toward tomorrow, the path forward shines brightly with limitless potential."*
- **Fix**: End on an actionable next step: an address, phone number, enquiry form, or specific schedule.

## 3. UI Design "AI Tells" to Eliminate

Wikipedia's analysis also extends to synthetic formatting tropes that plague AI-generated web interfaces:

1. **Floating Pill / Cylinder Badges everywhere**: Do not wrap every single section header, subtitle, or phrase in a rounded-full glowing cylinder container. Real websites use clean, well-spaced typography.
2. **Fake Reviews & Testimonials**: Fictional quotes attributed to stock avatars with generic corporate titles ("John Doe, VP at Global Corp") instantly destroy credibility. Ground trust in verifiable legal registries (MCA, RoC Patna, JustDial, Khatiyan).
3. **Overuse of Em Dashes (—)**: AI inserts em dashes in almost every sentence. Use natural sentence punctuation.
4. **Generic Cyber / Neon Gradients**: Do not use neon purple/cyan cyberpunk gradients for traditional, heritage, or local enterprises. Use native brand colors derived from real assets (e.g., warm imperial gold, brass bronze, and obsidian charcoal).
